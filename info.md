# Research Brief — Ariel's Coffee Dapto

## Identity & Facts
- **Name:** Ariel's Dapto — part of "Ariel's — My Local Café", a family-owned Illawarra café chain (est. 2015, 7 locations). The Dapto café opened **December 2022** inside Dapto Mall near Coles.
- **Address:** Ground Floor, Kiosk K105, Dapto Mall, 75–87 Princes Highway, Dapto NSW 2530
- **Phone:** 0414 329 909 · **Email:** hello@arielscoffee.com.au
- **Hours (official site):** Mon & Sun 8:00am–3:00pm · Tue–Sat 8:00am–4:00pm
- **Coffee:** award-winning **Campos Coffee**, La Marzocco machine; also "Australia's Favourite Chai"
- **Ordering:** Bopple (ariels.bopple.app/arielsdapto), Uber Eats, DoorDash, Hey You
- **Socials:** Instagram @arielsdapto (~187 followers, bio: "Ariel's @daptomall ☕ @Camposcoffee 🍳 All Day Breakfast 🥤 Smoothies 🌯 Burritos 🍔 Burgers") · Facebook /ArielsDapto (~314 likes, 278 check-ins)
- **Google Maps:** maps.app.goo.gl/BZKcT9zeDjAtWoPG9 — rating ~4.0★ on Google; **Uber Eats 4.5★ (180+)**, **DoorDash 4.6★ (200+)**
- **Price level:** $ (budget–moderate; most dishes $13–$21 AUD)
- **Brand motto:** "Honest food, honest coffee, honest people — that's the Illawarra way."
- **Community promos:** "Mum's Café Day" — free coffee for mums with prams every second Tuesday of the month.
- **Website status:** no Dapto-only domain exists (arielsdapto.com etc. are unregistered); only the chain site arielscoffee.com.au has a Dapto location page. This new site is a dedicated Dapto café website.

## Brand Visual Identity (from official site & physical kiosk)
- **Colors:** navy `#0d4074`, gold `#c0a34e`, bone/cream `#efede6`. Physical kiosk: navy counter with gold-trim panelling.
- **Logo:** "ARIEL'S — EST 2015" serif wordmark, white on navy with gold "EST 2015" underline. Navy branded takeaway cups & burrito wrap paper.
- **Typography:** elegant serif display (Cormorant Garamond style) + clean sans (Inter).
- **Photography modes:** (1) moody dark-marble coffee shots (rosetta latte art in grey ceramic); (2) bright overhead flat-lays on white marble/timber — generous, colourful breakfast spreads.
- **Note for design:** the user wants appetizing, appetite-stimulating coffee-foodie colors — blend the navy/gold/cream brand identity with warm, food-forward tones (caramel, cream, espresso) so the site whets appetites while staying on-brand.
