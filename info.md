Atmars is a made-up café used for this demo site. Every name, address, phone number, review, rating and photo is a placeholder; none of it refers to a real business.

- **Address:** Ground Floor, Kiosk K105, Null Island Mall, 1 Virtual Street, Null Island 0000

- **Ordering:** Bopple, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Hey You (links go to each platform's homepage)
- **Socials:** Instagram @atmars · Facebook /Atmars (placeholders, no real accounts)
- **Price level:** $ (most dishes $12–$23 AUD)
- **Motto:** "Honest food, honest coffee, honest people — that's the Atmars way."
- **Community promo:** "Mum's Café Day" — free coffee for mums with prams every second Tuesday of the month.

## Brand Visual Identity
- **Colors:** navy `#0d4074`, gold `#c0a34e`, bone/cream `#efede6`. The kiosk is a navy counter with gold-trim panelling.
- **Logo:** "ATMARS" serif wordmark over "Virtual Café · Est 2019". Navy takeaway cups and burrito wrap paper carry a small gold ATMARS wordmark.
- **Typography:** elegant serif display (Cormorant Garamond) + clean sans (Inter).
- **Photography modes:** (1) moody dark-marble coffee shots (rosetta latte art in grey ceramic); (2) bright overhead flat-lays on white marble/timber — generous, colourful breakfast spreads.
- **Design note:** blend the navy/gold/cream identity with warm, food-forward tones (caramel, cream, espresso) so the site whets appetites while staying on-brand.
